# MS 274 Project
Files for  MS 274 Project.

Project Title: 
The effects of El Nino Conditions on juvenile shiner surfperch

Project Description:
A strong El Nino event began developing in 2026, and is predicted to peak in late 2026 and persist through early 2027. While the California coast is typically highly productive, El Nino can reduce primary productivity in the coastal ocean (Chavez et al., 2002). In addition, El Nino can depress the thermocline, resulting in warmer surface temperatures. While reductions in primary productivity could alter prey availability for juvenile surfperch, warming temperatures may lead to faster developmental times and larger initial size at age (Fisher et al., 2015; Lomartire et al., 2021; Lindmark et al., 2022). This project will combine surf zone monitoring data from summer 2025 with summer 2026 field collections to capture juvenile shiner surfperch size in relation to environmental conditions at Stillwater Cove. Sea Surface Temperature (SST) will be estimated using MUR, and VIIRS will be used to estimate chlorophyll-a. Larger juvenile fish may have an advantage over smaller individuals due to decreased predation and higher tolerance of environmental changes (Sogard, 1997). Therefore, understanding how middle trophic level forage fish such as shiner surfperch respond to extreme environmental conditions can inform conservation planning and fisheries management.

Project Outline:

- Datasets
The Multi-scale Ultra-high Resolution (MUR) sea surface temperature (SST) dataset will be used to examine temperature at Stillwater Cove from June to September in 2025 and 2026. Visible Infrared Imaging Radiometer Suite (VIIRS) data will be used for chlorophyll-a estimates. Juvenile shiner surfperch size measurements from summer 2026 will be obtained from field collections, and summer 2025 measurements will be obtained from the California Marine Protected Area Surf Zone Monitoring Project.

- Figures
Proposed figures include SST from June-September in 2025 and 2026 at Stillwater Cove, Chlorophyll-a from June-September in 2025 and 2026 at Stillwater Cove, and juvenile shiner surfperch size versus temperature in 2025 and 2026 at Stillwater Cove.
